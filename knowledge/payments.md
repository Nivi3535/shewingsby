## What do the order statuses mean?
- **Pending**: the order has been received and is waiting to be confirmed.
- **Confirmed**: the order is confirmed and is being packed.
- **Shipped**: the order has been handed to the courier and is on its way.
- **Delivered**: the courier has delivered the order.
