## How do I measure my child?
Use a soft measuring tape over light clothing. Chest: measure around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms. Length: measure from the top of the shoulder down to where you want the outfit to end. For a pavadai (skirt), measure from the waist to the ankle.

## Do colours look exactly like the photos?
We try to show colours as accurately as possible, but silk and zari work can look slightly different under different lights and on different screens. A small colour difference is not treated as a defect.
